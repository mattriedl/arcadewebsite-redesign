# Copyright review list

This is a starting list of game pages whose names match well-known commercial games or franchises. It was made by matching names, so it will include some fan games, free demos or officially free versions, and it will miss some commercial games. It is not legal advice.

Why it matters for search: when a rights holder sends Google a valid copyright removal notice, Google removes that page from its results. Google also says sites with a high number of valid removal notices may be shown lower in search results overall, which can pull down every page on the domain, not just the reported ones.

Options for each game: keep it if you have the rights or it's a free official release; remove it; or keep it on the site but hide it from search engines by adding `"index": false` to that game in `data/games.json` and rebuilding.
