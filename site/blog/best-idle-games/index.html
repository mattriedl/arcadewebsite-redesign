What are idle games?

Idle games (also called clicker games or incremental games) are games where numbers go up, and then go up faster. You start by clicking to earn something, like cookies, coins or points. You spend it on helpers and upgrades that earn for you automatically, so the game keeps making progress even when you stop clicking. Most idle games share a few things:

  • One resource to grow: cookies, Dogecoin, capybaras or plain points.
  • Upgrades that stack: each purchase makes the next one come sooner.
  • Very simple controls: usually just the mouse, sometimes one key.
  • A reset for a bonus: many have a "prestige" or "ascend" button that starts you over with a permanent boost, so the next run goes faster.

They're a good fit for a browser tab you check on every few minutes, while you do something else.

The classic clicker games

1. Cookie Clicker

Cookie Clicker is the idle game most people know. It was made by Orteil, and the version on our site is 2.031. You start with a giant cookie, an empty bakery and a line on screen: "You feel like making cookies. But nobody wants to eat your cookies."

How to play: Click the big cookie to bake. Spend cookies in the Store on the right on buildings such as cursors, grandmas and farms, which bake cookies for you every second. The Buy 1, 10 and 100 buttons let you buy in bulk once you're rich.

Tip: Watch for golden cookies that flash up on the screen and click them fast; they give big bonuses for a short time. Later, the Legacy button lets you ascend, which resets your bakery but gives you heavenly chips and permanent upgrades. Your first ascension is worth it once your bakery slows down.

2. Capybara Clicker

Capybara Clicker is a simpler, friendlier take on the same idea, with a cheerful capybara in place of the cookie.

How to play: Click the capybara to earn capybaras. Buy the upgrades on the right to raise your Click Power and your Capybara Per Second. The Ascend button in the corner starts you over with a bigger Ascension Multiplier.

Tip: Early on, put most of your capybaras into the cheapest automatic upgrades. Ascend when the Ascend button shows a reward worth having, since the multiplier speeds up every run after.

3. Spacebar Clicker

Spacebar Clicker strips the genre down to one key. A big counter sits above a giant SPACEBAR button, with a "per second" number underneath.

How to play: Press the spacebar to add one to the counter. Once you have enough, spend your points on items that add points every second, so the counter keeps climbing on its own.

Tip: Click inside the game first so it catches your key presses. It's the quickest game on this list, and good for a two-minute break.

Best idle games with a twist

These games keep the "numbers go up" idea but add something else on top.

4. Idle Breakout

Idle Breakout mixes the arcade game Breakout with an idle game. It was made by Kodiqi and released in 2019. Instead of a paddle, you buy balls that bounce around and break numbered bricks on their own.

How to play: Click bricks to damage them and earn money, then buy and upgrade balls. There are several ball types: Basic, Plasma (hurts nearby bricks too), Sniper (aims at the nearest brick), Scatter (splits into smaller balls), Cannonball (smashes through rows) and Poison (damage over time). There are no lives and no way to lose.

Tip: Mix ball types instead of buying only one. Sniper balls clean up the last few bricks, which is usually what slows a level down. When progress stalls, prestige: you lose your balls but earn gold for permanent upgrades.

5. Idle Dice

Idle Dice is made by Luts91. You roll dice to earn points, then spend the points to make each roll worth more.

How to play: Click Roll (or let it roll on its own) and use Upgrade under a die to raise its level and multiplier. Buy more dice as you go. When several dice land on the same number, or you hit combos like three of a kind, a full house or a straight, your score gets multiplied.

Tip: Adding dice is usually worth more than upgrading one die again, because more dice means more combos. The Buy 1, 10, 100 and max buttons at the top save a lot of clicking later.

6. Revolution Idle

Revolution Idle is pure big-number fun. The opening screen says it plainly: watch the circles fill and the numbers go up.

How to play: Colored circles fill up on their own. Each time one fills, you earn score and add to the multiplier for that color. Spend money on buildings, which make the circles spin faster. Each building has its own Ascend button, a prestige that resets it for a bigger boost. The side menu has Achievements, Statistics and Time Flux, and a bar at the bottom tracks your progress to Infinity.

Tip: Read the pop-up hints in your first few minutes; they explain each new feature as it unlocks. Revolution Idle is all about stacking multipliers, so keep every color's circle moving rather than pouring everything into one.

Idle games with a story or a theme

7. A Dark Room

A Dark Room is one of the most famous incremental games ever made. It's a text game by Doublespeak Games. It opens with just two lines: "the fire is dead. the room is freezing."

How to play: Click "light fire," then keep clicking "stoke fire" to keep it going. A stranger arrives and helps you, you start gathering wood and building, and the game slowly opens up into something much bigger. We won't spoil where it goes.

Tip: Be patient in the first few minutes, since it starts slowly on purpose. It saves as you play, and the menu at the bottom has a "lights off" option and many languages. Play it with sound on if you can.

8. Doge Miner 2

Doge Miner 2 is a mining clicker starring the Shiba Inu dog from the Doge meme, made by rkn.

How to play: Click the rock to mine Dogecoin. Spend it in the Shop on helpers like Mining Shibes and Doge Kennels, and save up for the Space Rocket, which takes your mining off Earth.

Tip: Buy the cheap Mining Shibes early so you're earning while you click. Check the Upgrades tab as well as the Shop, since upgrades often give a bigger boost than another helper.

9. Idle Idle Gamedev

Idle Idle Gamedev is also by Luts91. You run a tiny game studio that makes idle games, which is where the double "idle" comes from.

How to play: Click Think to come up with ideas, develop your game, then release it to earn players. Each game has stats such as Quality, Retention and Playtime, and you spend Creativity Points on upgrades.

Tip: This one is more of a light management game than a clicker, so read the stats on each release. The "Watch Ad for 2x Speed" button is optional; you can ignore it.

How to pick the best idle game for you

  • New to idle games? Start with Cookie Clicker. Almost every other clicker borrows its ideas.
  • Get bored of just clicking? Idle Breakout and Idle Dice give you something to watch.
  • Want a story? A Dark Room is the one.
  • Only have a few minutes? Spacebar Clicker or Capybara Clicker.

You can find more in our idle and clicker games collection. If idle games aren't quite your thing, the puzzle games category has slower, thinking games like 2048, and our list of games to play when bored covers every mood.

A few tips for any clicker game

  • Buy automation first. Anything that earns per second is worth more than a stronger click, because it works while you're away.
  • Don't fear the reset. Prestige, ascend and similar buttons feel like losing, but the permanent bonus makes the next run much faster.
  • Your save lives in your browser. These games save progress in the browser you play in. Clearing your browser data or switching computers usually means starting over.
  • Take breaks. Idle games are built to be checked on, not stared at. Leave the tab open and come back later.

Frequently asked questions

What is the best idle game to play in a browser?

Cookie Clicker is the best place to start, because it's simple, free and the game most other clickers copy. For something different, Idle Breakout and A Dark Room are two of the most popular browser idle games.

What are idle games?

Idle games are games where you earn a resource, spend it on upgrades and helpers, and then those helpers keep earning for you automatically. You make progress even when you aren't clicking, which is why they're also called incremental games.

What is the difference between idle games and clicker games?

Clicker games focus on clicking to earn, while idle games focus on progress that happens on its own. Most games, like Cookie Clicker, are both: you click at first, then automation takes over.

What are some games like Cookie Clicker?

Capybara Clicker, Doge Miner 2 and Spacebar Clicker work the same way as Cookie Clicker: click, buy helpers, grow. Idle Breakout and Revolution Idle keep the same upgrade loop with a different style of play.

Do idle games keep playing when the tab is closed?

It depends on the game. Some, like Idle Breakout, give you offline progress when you come back, while others only earn while the tab is open. Either way, your progress is saved in your browser.

Are these idle games free?

Yes. All nine games on this list are free to play on ArcadeWebsite, with no download or account needed.
