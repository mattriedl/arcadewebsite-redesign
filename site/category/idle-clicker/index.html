About our idle/clicker games

Idle and clicker games are about watching numbers grow. Click to earn, buy upgrades and helpers, and let your empire keep producing even when you step away.

Start with Cookie Clicker, the game that made the genre famous, then try Doge Miner, Idle Breakout and more.

Popular idle/clicker games include Cookie Clicker, Doge Miner, Factory Balls, Idle Breakout, Learn to Fly Idle.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Idle/Clicker games FAQ

What is an idle game?

An idle game keeps progressing on its own once you've bought upgrades, so you earn even while you're not clicking.

Do idle games save my progress?

Most idle games save progress in your browser automatically. Clearing your browser data will reset it.
