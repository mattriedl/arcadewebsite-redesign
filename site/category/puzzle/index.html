About our puzzle games

Puzzle games make you think. Merge numbers, roll blocks into holes, sort colors, guess words and solve brain teasers that start easy and get tricky fast.

Classics like 2048 and Wordle sit alongside block puzzles, logic games and daily-style word games you can play in a few minutes.

Popular puzzle games include 2048, Block Blast, The Impossible Quiz, The Impossible Quiz 2, Riddle School, Riddle School 2.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Puzzle games FAQ

What are the most popular puzzle games?

2048, Wordle, Bloxorz and Block Blast are some of the most played puzzle games here.

Are puzzle games good for your brain?

Puzzle games exercise planning, pattern recognition and problem solving, and many people play them to relax.
