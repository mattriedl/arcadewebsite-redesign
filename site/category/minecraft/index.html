Minecraft games FAQ

Can I play Minecraft in my browser?

The Eaglercraft games here are fan-made browser versions of Minecraft that run without installing anything.

Do Minecraft browser games save my world?

Worlds are usually saved in your browser, so use the same browser to come back to them.
