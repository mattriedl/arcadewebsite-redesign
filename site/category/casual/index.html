About our casual games

Casual games are relaxed and easy to pick up, perfect for a short break. Drop fruit, stack blocks, draw lines, bounce balls and solve light puzzles at your own pace.

These games are simple to learn, quick to play and don't need fast reflexes, though many have a high score to beat.

Popular casual games include Papa Louie: When Pizzas Attack!, Papa's Pizzeria, Papa's Burgeria, Red Ball, Red Ball 4, Snake.is.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Casual games FAQ

What is a casual game?

A casual game has simple rules and short sessions, so anyone can start playing right away.

Are casual games good for a quick break?

Yes. Most casual games can be played in a few minutes and picked up again any time.
