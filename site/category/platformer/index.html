About our platformer games

Platformer games are all about running, jumping and timing. Climb towers, dodge spikes and saw blades, wall-jump through tight corridors and race to the exit in some of the best-known platform games on the web.

You'll find precision challenges like Big Tower Tiny Square and Vex, speedy runners like Run 3 and OvO, and co-op puzzlers like Fireboy and Watergirl that you can play with a friend on one keyboard.

Popular platformer games include Cat Mario, Fancy Pants Adventure, Geometry Dash Lite, Helix Jump, Run, Run 2.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Platformer games FAQ

What is a platformer game?

A platformer is a game where you move a character across platforms, jumping over gaps and obstacles to reach the end of each level.

Can I play platformer games with a friend?

Yes. Games like Fireboy and Watergirl let two players share one keyboard.
