About our battle games

Battle games put you head to head with an opponent, whether it's a friend on the same keyboard or the computer. Fight with fists, swords, guns or tanks in fast rounds that are easy to start and hard to master.

Many of these games support two players on one keyboard, making them perfect for a quick match with a friend.

Popular battle games include Friday Night Funkin', Hole.io, Paper.io, Paper.io 3D, Slither.io, Stack Ball.io.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Battle games FAQ

Can two people play battle games on one computer?

Many of them, yes. Look for two-player games where each player uses a different side of the keyboard.

Do I need to download anything?

No. All battle games play directly in your browser.
