About our shooting games

Shooting games test your aim and reflexes. Take on waves of enemies, snipe from rooftops, defend your base or build and fight in fast online-style duels.

This collection includes top-down shooters, side-scrolling action games, sniper challenges and classic first-person shooters you can play in your browser.

Popular shooting games include 1v1.LOL, Bubble Shooter, Bubble Tanks, Bubble Tanks 2, Getaway Shootout, Gun Mayhem.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Shooting games FAQ

What kinds of shooting games are there?

You'll find first-person shooters, top-down and side-scrolling shooters, sniper games, tank games and defense games.

How do I aim in shooting games?

Most shooting games use the mouse to aim and click to shoot, with WASD or the arrow keys to move. Each game shows its controls.
