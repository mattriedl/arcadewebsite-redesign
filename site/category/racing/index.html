About our racing games

Racing games let you drift around corners, launch off ramps and race against the clock. Drive stunt bikes, supercars, monster trucks and even a car with an egg on top.

From stunt series like Moto X3M to drifting games like Drift Hunters and physics challenges like Drive Mad, there's a racing game for every kind of driver.

Popular racing games include Drift Boss, Drift Hunters, Drive Mad, Eggy Car, Happy Wheels, Madalin Stunt Cars.

Every game on this page is free and plays in your browser. Can't find what you want? Try the full A–Z list or another category.

Racing games FAQ

What are the best racing games to start with?

Moto X3M, Drift Hunters, Drive Mad and Madalin Stunt Cars are popular places to start.

Do racing games work with the keyboard?

Yes. Most racing games use the arrow keys or WASD to drive.
