## What are io games?

Io games are simple online arena games that run in a web browser. The name comes from Agar.io, a 2015 game whose web address ended in ".io", and many games that copied its idea kept the ending. Most io games share a few things:

- **One simple goal:** eat, capture or knock out others to get bigger or score more.
- **Easy controls:** usually just the mouse, or the mouse plus a key or two.
- **Short rounds:** when you lose, you start again from small in a few seconds.
- **A leaderboard:** the biggest players are shown on screen, which gives you something to aim for.

## The classic io games

These are the games most people mean when they say "io games." Each one takes about ten seconds to learn.

### 1. Slither.io

[Slither.io](/game/slither-io/) is a snake game. You eat glowing orbs to grow longer, and if another snake runs its head into your body, it crashes and leaves behind a trail of food you can eat.

**How to play:** Steer with the mouse. Click or hold the mouse button to speed up, which costs a little of your length. Before you start, the version on our site lets you pick a skin and a room size (small, medium or large).

**Tip:** The best move is to cut in front of a bigger snake so its head hits your body, then eat what it drops. Only boost when you need to, because boosting shrinks you. Once you're long, circle around smaller snakes to trap them.

### 2. Paper.io

[Paper.io](/game/paper-io/) is a territory game. You leave your colored area to draw a line, then return home to claim everything inside the loop. If another player crosses your line while you're outside, you're out. The version on our site is Paper.io 2, made by Voodoo.

**How to play:** Steer with the mouse, WASD or the arrow keys. Loop back to your own color to capture land.

**Tip:** Make small, quick loops early on. A long trail is a long target. When a rival leaves their area, you can cut their line to knock them out and take over their land.

### 3. Hole.io

[Hole.io](/game/hole-io/) puts you in charge of a hole in the ground in a busy city. Move under objects smaller than you to swallow them, and you get bigger. It's made by Voodoo.

**How to play:** Move the hole with the mouse and swallow as much as you can before the timer runs out.

**Tip:** Start with small things like people, cones and benches. Each one makes you a little bigger, so after a while you can swallow cars, then whole buildings. Don't waste time near objects that are still too big for you.

### 4. Agar.io Lite

[Agar.io Lite](/game/agar-iolite/) is a simple take on Agar.io, the game that started the io craze. You're a circle on a big map. The start screen sums it up: eat smaller circles and avoid bigger ones.

**How to play:** Click to begin, then move the mouse to steer your circle.

**Tip:** Keep your eyes on the circles around you, not just the food. While you're small, steer away from anything bigger as soon as it appears on screen, and only chase circles you're sure are smaller than you.

If you like these, [Snowball.io](/game/snowball-io/) and [Cleanup.io](/game/cleanup-io/) further down use the same "start small, grow big" idea.

## IO games with more action

These io games add weapons, building or fighting.

### 5. Smash Karts

[Smash Karts](/game/smash-karts/) is a kart battle game by Tall Team. You drive around an arena, pick up weapons from item boxes and blast the other karts. Matches last about 3 minutes.

**How to play:** Drive with WASD or the arrow keys and press space to fire a weapon.

**Tip:** Keep moving. A kart that stops is an easy target. Grab every item box you pass, and save your shot until you're close and lined up behind someone. Smash Karts was also in our list of [games to play when bored](/blog/games-to-play-when-bored/).

### 6. Surviv.io

[Surviv.io](/game/surve-vio/) is a 2D battle royale seen from above. Everyone drops onto an island, looks for weapons and gear, and the last player or team left wins. The original Surviv.io came out in 2017. The version on our site is Survev.io, an open-source recreation of it.

**How to play:** The game's own "How to Play" screen lists the controls: move with W, A, S and D, aim with the mouse and left-click to shoot or hit. Press 1 to 4 or scroll to switch weapons, R to reload and F to pick up loot. On the start screen you choose a region and play Solo, Duo or Squad.

**Tip:** Loot a few buildings before you go looking for fights. Better gear makes a big difference, and the players who rush into the open early are usually the first ones out.

### 7. 1v1.LOL

[1v1.LOL](/game/1v1-lol/) is a third-person building and shooting game. You build walls, ramps and floors in a second to get the high ground, then out-aim your opponent.

**How to play:** Move with WASD, aim and shoot with the mouse, and press Z, X, C and V (the default keys) to build. The start screen offers modes including 1v1, Box 2v2, Battle Royale Duos, Party, Zombies and an Aim Trainer.

**Tip:** Start in Free Build or the Aim Trainer. Building fast is the biggest skill in this game, and it's easier to learn without someone shooting at you.

## IO strategy games

### 8. Territorial.io

[Territorial.io](/game/territorial-io/) is a real-time strategy game. You start as a small nation on a map and grow by taking empty land, then by attacking your neighbors. The menu has a Multiplayer mode and a Custom Scenario mode.

**How to play:** Use the slider to choose how many of your troops to send, then click where you want to expand or attack.

**Tip:** Don't send all your troops at once. A nation that spends everything on one attack has nothing left to defend its borders. Grab empty land first, and attack neighbors when they're busy fighting someone else.

## Easy io games for younger players

### 9. Snowball.io

[Snowball.io](/game/snowball-io/) is an arena game on a round island of ice. You make snowballs and throw them at the other players, and the last player standing wins.

**How to play:** Hold the left mouse button to move and build a snowball, then let go to shoot it.

**Tip:** Keep moving while you build your snowball, so you're harder to hit, and wait until a rival is close and lined up before you let go. A missed shot leaves you open.

### 10. Cleanup.io

[Cleanup.io](/game/cleanup-io/) is a lot like Hole.io, but you're a robot vacuum cleaner in an office. Vacuum up papers and small things to grow, then move on to bigger objects until you can clean the whole map. It's made by BinStudio.

**How to play:** Hold the left mouse button and drag to steer the vacuum.

**Tip:** Like in Hole.io, go after the small things first. Each piece you pick up makes you bigger, so you can take on larger furniture sooner.

## How to get better at any io game

Most io games reward the same habits:

1. **Play safe while you're small.** Early on, almost everyone can beat you. Collect easy points before you take risks.
2. **Watch the leaderboard and the edges of the screen.** Big players coming toward you are your biggest danger.
3. **Use speed boosts carefully.** In games like Slither.io, boosting costs size, so use it to escape or to cut someone off, not just to move around.
4. **Don't get greedy.** In Paper.io and Territorial.io, the biggest losses come from going too far from home.
5. **Restart fast.** Losing is part of every io game. A new round takes seconds, and you learn something every time.

For most players, Slither.io or Paper.io is the best place to start, because you can learn them in seconds and a round takes a few minutes. If you want more action, Smash Karts and Surviv.io are the best picks on this list.

### What are io games?

Io games are simple online arena games that run in a browser, usually with easy mouse controls and short rounds. The name comes from the ".io" web address of Agar.io, the game that made the style popular.

### What are the most popular io games?

Slither.io, Paper.io, Agar.io and Hole.io are among the best-known io games. All four, plus the other games in this list, are free to play on ArcadeWebsite.

Agar.io Lite, Paper.io and Hole.io all use the same idea of starting small and growing by eating or capturing. Snowball.io and Cleanup.io are good, simpler picks for younger players.

### Are io games free?

Yes. Every io game on this list is free to play in your browser, with no download or account needed. Some games have optional skins, but you don't need them to play.
