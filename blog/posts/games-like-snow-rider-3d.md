## What makes Snow Rider 3D fun (and how to play it)

If you haven't played it yet, [Snow Rider 3D](/game/snow-rider/) puts you on a wooden sled racing down an endless snowy hill. Trees, snowmen and other obstacles come at you, and one hit ends the run. Gifts lie along the way, and collecting them raises your score.

**Controls:** The in-game tutorial shows the left and right arrow keys to steer, and the up arrow to jump. Its one warning is "Don't forget to jump through the obstacles!", and that's the main thing new players miss.

**Tip:** Steer early and in small taps. Holding an arrow key down swings the sled a long way, which is how most runs end in a tree. If one copy of the game won't start for you, try [Snow Rider 3D (Version 3)](/game/snow-rider-rrr/), a second copy on the site.

The games below keep that feeling but change one thing each: the world, the speed, or what you're riding.

## 1. Snow Road: the closest game to Snow Rider 3D

[Snow Road](/game/snow-road/) is the most similar game on the site. You ride a sled downhill through a blocky, voxel-style winter world with snowy pine trees and houses, picking up gifts as you go. The start screen even uses the same wooden sign for your best score and number of plays, plus a shop and settings button.

**Controls:** Press Space on the title screen and again on the sign to start a run. Steer with the left and right arrow keys. When you crash, a screen shows your score, your best score and how many gifts you collected.

**Tip:** The track has walls and cliffs along the sides, and steering too far into them ends your run just like hitting a tree. Stay near the middle and only move over when something is in your way.

## 2. Slope: the best for pure speed

[Slope](/game/slope/) swaps the snow for a neon track. You steer a ball down the slope, and it gets faster the longer you survive. Gaps, ramps and red blocks appear without warning, so it tests your reflexes the same way Snow Rider 3D does.

**Controls:** Use the left and right arrow keys, or A and D, to roll the ball. Stay on the green track and avoid the red obstacles.

**Tip:** Look further down the track than you think you need to. The ball builds up speed fast, so by the time a gap is right in front of you it's often too late to turn.

## 3. Tunnel Rush: the best for fast reflexes

[Tunnel Rush](/game/tunnel-rush-better/) sends you racing down a colorful, rotating tunnel full of walls, bars and spinning blades. There's no snow, but the idea is the same as Snow Rider 3D: keep moving, dodge everything, and see how far you get. The screen shows your distance in meters and your speed, and a "New record" screen pops up when you beat your best.

**Controls:** Use the left and right arrow keys, or A and D, to rotate around the tunnel. The game shows the arrow keys on screen when you start.

**Tip:** Each obstacle has a gap. Find the gap first and rotate toward it, instead of reacting to the part that's blocked. Short taps work better than holding a key.

## 4. Moto X3M Winter: the best snowy game with levels

[Moto X3M Winter](/game/moto-x3m-winter/) is the snowy edition of the Moto X3M stunt bike series, with icy ramps and Christmas-themed traps. Instead of one endless hill, you get short levels with a finish line, so it's a good pick when you want to beat something rather than just survive.

**Controls:** Ride with the arrow keys. As in the original [Moto X3M](/game/moto-x3m/), up accelerates, down brakes, and left and right lean your bike back and forward so you can land safely and do flips.

**Tip:** Flipping in the air cuts time off the clock, but a bad landing costs you more. Learn each level slowly first, then go for flips once you know where the jumps are. You'll find more bike and driving games in the [racing category](/category/racing/).

## 5. Line Rider: draw your own sled track

[Line Rider](/game/line-rider/) is the classic drawing game by Boštjan Čadež, whose name is on the title screen. Instead of steering, you draw the hill. A little rider on a sled then rides down whatever you've made, flying off jumps and tumbling off if the track throws him too hard.

**Controls:** The game's own Quick Start explains it. Use the pencil tool to draw a line from the top left towards the bottom right, then press the play button and watch the rider go. The stop button brings you back to the track editor, and pressing H in the editor shows more help.

**Tip:** Start with one long, gentle slope and test it before adding jumps. Small changes to the angle make a big difference to how far the rider flies.

## 6. Run 3: the best for long sessions

[Run 3](/game/run-3/) has a small grey alien running through tunnels floating in space. When you run onto a wall, the whole tunnel rotates so the wall becomes the floor. It has an endless mode like Snow Rider 3D, plus a long story mode with hand-built levels and characters to unlock, so it lasts much longer than a single run.

**Controls:** Use the arrow keys to move and jump.

**Tip:** When a gap is too wide to jump, run onto the side wall instead. The tunnel turns with you, and you can often skip the gap completely.

## 7. Slope 2 Player: race a friend

[Slope 2 Player](/game/slope2-player/) puts two balls on the same speeding track, so you can race a friend on one keyboard. Whoever stays on the course longest wins the round.

**Controls:** One player steers with A and D, and the other with the left and right arrow keys.

**Tip:** Don't watch the other player's ball. It's the fastest way to fall off. If you like playing side by side, our list of the [best 2 player games on one keyboard](/blog/best-2-player-games-one-keyboard/) has more.

## Tips for getting better at sledding and runner games

These games all reward the same habits:

1. **Look ahead, not at your character.** Your eyes should be on the next obstacle, not the one you're passing.
2. **Tap, don't hold.** Small taps on the arrow keys keep you in control. Holding a key down usually overcorrects.
3. **Use fullscreen.** A bigger screen gives you more time to see what's coming. Every game page on the site has a fullscreen button.
4. **Take breaks.** If you keep crashing at the same spot, step away for a few minutes. Reaction games are much harder when you're frustrated.
