## Quick games to play when bored (5 minutes or less)

You can learn these in seconds, and a round ends fast.

### 1. Slope

[Slope](/game/slope/) is an endless 3D running game. You steer a ball down a neon track that gets faster the longer you survive. Gaps, ramps and red blocks appear without warning, so every run tests your reflexes.

**How to play:** Use the left and right arrow keys, or A and D, to roll the ball. Stay on the green track, avoid the red obstacles and see how far you get before you fall off the edge.

**Tip:** Look further down the track than feels natural. The ball speeds up quickly, so by the time a gap is right in front of you, it's often too late to turn. Short taps beat holding a key down. If you like Slope, our list of [games like Snow Rider 3D](/blog/games-like-snow-rider-3d/) has more games with the same "steer and don't crash" feel.

### 2. Drift Boss

[Drift Boss](/game/drift-boss/) is a one-button driving game. Your car drives along a twisting track on its own, and your only job is to drift around each corner without falling off.

**How to play:** Click or press any key to turn, and let go to drive straight.

**Tip:** It's all about timing. Start each turn a moment before the corner, and keep your turns short until you get a feel for the track.

### 3. Paper.io

[Paper.io](/game/paper-io/) is a territory game. You leave your colored area to draw a line, then return home to claim everything inside the loop. Other players can cut your line while you're outside, so every trip is a choice between greed and safety.

**How to play:** Steer with the mouse or the arrow keys. Loop back to your own color to capture land.

**Tip:** Make small, quick loops early on. A long trail is a long target, and if anyone crosses it before you get home, your round is over.

## Brain games for when you're bored

These two are calm, with no timer pushing you.

### 4. 2048

[2048](/game/2048/) is the number puzzle Gabriele Cirulli released in 2014. You slide numbered tiles on a 4×4 grid, and when two tiles with the same number touch, they merge into one. Keep combining until you build a 2048 tile, or keep going for a higher score.

**How to play:** Use the arrow keys, or swipe on a touch screen, to move every tile at once.

**Tip:** Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile there. Try to use only three directions and avoid the fourth unless you have no choice, so the big tile stays put.

### 5. Solitaire

[Solitaire](/game/solitaire/) is the classic card game. You build four foundation piles, one for each suit, from ace to king by moving cards between the columns.

**How to play:** Drag cards with the mouse, or click the deck to draw new cards.

**Tip:** Move cards from the columns before you draw from the deck, and try to uncover the face-down cards in the biggest columns first. New to the game? Our guide on [how to play Solitaire](/blog/how-to-play-solitaire/) covers the setup, rules and more tips. ## Idle games that keep you busy

Idle games keep making progress even when you stop clicking. Start one, check back later and spend what you earned.

### 6. Cookie Clicker

[Cookie Clicker](/game/cookie-clicker-cool/) is the idle game that made clicking famous, created by the French developer Orteil in 2013. You click a giant cookie to bake, then spend your cookies on cursors, grandmas, farms and factories that bake even more for you.

**How to play:** Click the big cookie to start, then buy buildings and upgrades from the store on the right. Your bakery keeps producing while you watch.

**Tip:** Don't spend everything on buildings. Upgrades can boost the buildings you already own, so check the upgrades in the store each time you have cookies to spend.

### 7. Tiny Fishing

[Tiny Fishing](/game/tiny-fishing/) is an idle fishing game. You cast your line, catch as many fish as you can on the way back up and spend your earnings on better gear.

**How to play:** Click to cast and move the mouse to steer the hook. Spend your earnings on longer lines and better hooks.

**Tip:** Put your first upgrades into line length. A longer line lets your hook go deeper, so you pass more fish on the way back up. For more games like these, browse the [idle and clicker category](/category/idle-clicker/), including [Idle Breakout](/game/idle-breakout/), which mixes brick breaking with idle upgrades.

## Online games to play when bored

These two put you in an arena with other players, so no two rounds are the same.

### 8. Slither.io

[Slither.io](/game/slither-io/) is a snake game where you eat glowing orbs to grow longer. If another snake crashes into your body, it's out, and you can eat what it leaves behind.

**How to play:** Steer with the mouse. Click or hold the mouse button to speed up, which costs you some length.

**Tip:** Stay small and careful at the start. Boosting is how you trap other snakes, but it also shrinks you, so save it for when a snake is about to hit your body. More games like this are in the [battle category](/category/battle/), such as [Hole.io](/game/hole-io/), where you swallow a city as a hole in the ground.

### 9. Smash Karts

[Smash Karts](/game/smash-karts/), made by the studio Tall Team, is an online kart battle game. You drive around an arena, pick up weapons from boxes and blast the other karts.

**How to play:** Drive with the arrow keys or WASD and press space to fire the weapon you're holding.

**Tip:** Keep moving and drive through as many weapon boxes as you can. A kart that stops to aim is an easy target. It needs a working internet connection, since you play against people online.

## Games for a longer session

When you've got an afternoon to fill, these two have lots of levels to work through.

### 10. Moto X3M

[Moto X3M](/game/moto-x3m/), by MadPuffers, is a stunt bike racing game with short levels full of loops, ramps, saws and explosives. Finish fast to earn three stars, and flip in the air to take time off the clock.

**How to play:** Press the up arrow to accelerate and the down arrow to brake or reverse. Use the left and right arrows to lean back and forward, so you land on your wheels and can do flips.

**Tip:** Play each new level slowly once to learn where the traps are, then go back for flips and a better time. More driving games are in the [racing category](/category/racing/).

### 11. Run 3

[Run 3](/game/run-3/), by Joseph Cloutier, sends a small grey alien running through tunnels floating in space. When you run onto a wall, the whole tunnel rotates and the wall becomes the floor. It has an endless mode plus a long story mode of hand-built levels with characters to unlock.

**How to play:** Use the arrow keys to move and jump. Run onto a wall to rotate the tunnel.

**Tip:** When a gap is too wide to jump, look for a wall to run onto instead. Rotating around the tunnel is often the safer path.

## Something to play with a friend

### 12. Fireboy and Watergirl

[Fireboy and Watergirl](/game/fireboy-and-water-girl/) in the Forest Temple is a co-op puzzle platformer. You guide both characters to their exits: Fireboy can cross lava but not water, Watergirl can cross water but not lava, and green goo stops them both.

**How to play:** Fireboy moves with the arrow keys and Watergirl with W, A and D. Play with a friend on one keyboard, or control both characters yourself, and collect the gems on the way.

**Tip:** Talk before you move. Many rooms need one player to stand on a button or hold a lever while the other gets through. For more games two people can share one keyboard, see our list of the [best 2 player games on one keyboard](/blog/best-2-player-games-one-keyboard/).

## How to pick a game when you're bored

If you can't decide, match the game to the time you have and your mood:

- **A few minutes:** Drift Boss, Slope or a round of Paper.io. You'll know within a minute if you like it.
- **Want to relax:** Solitaire or 2048. Neither has a clock rushing you.
- **Doing something else at the same time:** Cookie Clicker or Tiny Fishing. Check back now and then to buy upgrades.
- **Want to beat other people:** Slither.io or Smash Karts.
- **Have a friend next to you:** Fireboy and Watergirl.
- **A whole afternoon:** Run 3 or Moto X3M, which have many levels to finish.

If none of these fit, the [arcade category](/category/arcade/) has hundreds more quick games to try.

## Frequently asked questions

It depends on your mood, but Slope and Paper.io are good first picks because a round takes only a couple of minutes. To relax instead, try 2048 or Solitaire.

### What can I play when I only have 5 minutes?

Try Drift Boss, Slope or Paper.io. Each round is short, and you can restart instantly when you lose.
