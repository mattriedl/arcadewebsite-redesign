## Keyboard-only games for Chromebook

These are the easiest games to play on a Chromebook, because you never touch the trackpad once the game starts. Every Chromebook keyboard has arrow keys, and that's all you need.

### 1. Slope

[Slope](/game/slope/) is an endless 3D running game. You steer a ball down a neon track that gets faster the longer you survive, while gaps, ramps and red blocks appear without warning.

**Controls:** Left and right arrow keys, or A and D, roll the ball. Stay on the green track and avoid the red obstacles.

**Chromebook tip:** Rest two fingers on the arrow keys before you start so your hand doesn't move during the run. Tap instead of holding a key, because small corrections keep the ball centered at high speed.

### 2. Run 3

[Run 3](/game/run-3/), made by Joseph Cloutier, sends a small grey alien running through tunnels floating in space. When you run onto a wall, the whole tunnel rotates and that wall becomes the floor. It has an endless mode and a long series of hand-built levels with characters to unlock.

**Controls:** Arrow keys to move and jump.

**Chromebook tip:** Run 3 is a good pick when you have more time, because the level mode lets you make progress a few tunnels at a time. If a gap looks too wide to jump, try running onto the side wall instead.

### 3. Moto X3M

[Moto X3M](/game/moto-x3m/) is a stunt bike racing game with short levels full of loops, ramps, saws and explosives. Finish quickly to earn three stars, and do flips in the air to cut time off the clock.

**Controls:** Up arrow to accelerate, down arrow to brake or reverse, and left and right arrows to lean back and forward.

**Chromebook tip:** Most crashes come from landing at the wrong angle. Lean with the left and right arrows while you're in the air so both wheels touch down together. Levels are short, so restarting costs you only a few seconds.

### 4. 2048

[2048](/game/2048/) is the number puzzle Gabriele Cirulli released in 2014. You slide numbered tiles on a 4×4 grid, and when two tiles with the same number touch, they merge. Keep combining until you build a 2048 tile, or keep going for a higher score.

**Controls:** Arrow keys move every tile at once. On a touch screen, you can swipe.

**Chromebook tip:** Pick one corner and keep your biggest tile there. Mostly use just two or three directions, and only press the fourth when you have no other move. 2048 is also one of the lightest games here, so it runs well even on older Chromebooks.

## One-button games that work on any trackpad

One-button games are perfect for a Chromebook: you can play with a single key, a click or a tap on the trackpad.

### 5. Drift Boss

[Drift Boss](/game/drift-boss/) is a one-button driving game. Your car drives along a twisting track on its own, and your only job is to drift around each corner without falling off the edge.

**Controls:** Click or press any key to turn, and release to drive straight.

**Chromebook tip:** Use the space bar instead of the trackpad. A key gives you a cleaner press and release than a trackpad click, and timing is the whole game.

### 6. Space Waves

[Space Waves](/game/space-waves/) is a rhythm-style arcade game where you guide a zigzagging wave through tight neon corridors. The game's opening tutorial walks you through the controls before the real levels start.

**Controls:** Hold the mouse button, W, space or the up arrow to fly up, and let go to drop down.

**Chromebook tip:** Holding a key is much easier than holding down a trackpad click for a long stretch. Use space or the up arrow, and keep your presses short in narrow sections.

## Slow-paced Chromebook games for trackpad players

These games use the mouse, but nothing happens fast, so a trackpad works just as well. They're also nice when you want something calmer.

### 7. Chess

[Chess](/game/chess/) is the classic strategy board game. You can play against the computer, and the start menu also has a two-player mode for sharing one screen.

**Controls:** Click a piece, then click the square you want to move it to.

**Chromebook tip:** Since each move is just two clicks, a trackpad tap is all you need. If you're new to board games, our guide on [how to play checkers](/blog/how-to-play-checkers/) is a gentler place to start before chess.

### 8. Idle Breakout

[Idle Breakout](/game/idle-breakout/) mixes brick breaking with an idle game. You buy balls that bounce around and break bricks on their own, then upgrade them to clear levels faster.

**Controls:** Click bricks to damage them, and use the money you earn to buy and upgrade balls.

**Chromebook tip:** You don't need to click fast. Buy a few balls early so the game earns money for you, then spend your clicks on the last stubborn bricks of each level. For more games like this, see our list of the [best idle games](/blog/best-idle-games/).

## Games for two players on one Chromebook

Both of these games let two people share one keyboard, so a single Chromebook is enough for a match.

### 9. Fireboy and Watergirl

[Fireboy and Watergirl in the Forest Temple](/game/fireboy-and-water-girl/), created by Oslo Albet, is a co-op puzzle platformer. Fireboy can cross lava but not water, Watergirl can cross water but not lava, and green goo stops both of them. You need to get both characters to their exits.

**Controls:** Fireboy moves with the arrow keys, and Watergirl with W, A and D. You can play with a friend or control both characters yourself.

**Chromebook tip:** Chromebook keyboards are small, so sit side by side with one person on the left keys and one on the right. Talk through each room before moving, since many puzzles need one character to hold a button while the other passes.

### 10. Basket Random

[Basket Random](/game/basket-random/) is a silly two-player basketball game. Floppy players, changing courts and random physics make every point unpredictable.

**Controls:** Each player uses one key to jump and shoot: W for player one and the up arrow for player two.

**Chromebook tip:** With only one key each, Basket Random is the easiest two-player game to teach someone in seconds. Our list of [2 player games on one keyboard](/blog/best-2-player-games-one-keyboard/) has more games like it.

## Online games to play on a Chromebook

These games put you against other players over the internet. They need a steady connection, and 3D games like Smash Karts ask more of your Chromebook than simple 2D games.

### 11. Paper.io

[Paper.io](/game/paper-io/) is a territory game. You leave your colored area to draw a line, then return home to claim everything inside your loop. If another player crosses your line while you're outside, you're out.

**Controls:** Steer with the arrow keys or the mouse. Loop back to your own color to capture land.

**Chromebook tip:** Use the arrow keys rather than the trackpad, because sharp turns are much easier with a key press. Make small loops early so your trail is never long.

### 12. Smash Karts

[Smash Karts](/game/smash-karts/) is a multiplayer kart battle game. You drive around, collect weapons from boxes and blast other players.

**Controls:** Drive with the arrow keys or WASD, and press space to fire.

**Chromebook tip:** Since it's 3D, Smash Karts is the game on this list most likely to feel slow on an older model. Close other tabs before you play, and if it still stutters, switch to a 2D game like Paper.io. Our list of the [best io games](/blog/best-io-games/) has more online picks.

## How to get the best game performance on a Chromebook

A Chromebook runs ChromeOS and does most things through the Chrome browser, which is exactly why browser games suit it. A few habits help them run smoothly:

1. **Close tabs you aren't using.** Every open tab uses memory, and games run better with more of it free.
2. **Use the fullscreen button.** Our game pages have a fullscreen button under the game, which gives you a bigger view and fewer distractions.
3. **Reload if a game freezes.** The reload button on the game page restarts the game without leaving the page.
4. **Pick 2D games on older Chromebooks.** Puzzle, idle and 2D arcade games like 2048, Drift Boss and Idle Breakout need less power than 3D games.
5. **Plug in a mouse for aiming games.** Keyboard and one-button games are fine on a trackpad, but if you get into games where you aim, a cheap USB or Bluetooth mouse helps a lot.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can you play games on a Chromebook?

Yes. Any game that runs in the Chrome browser works on a Chromebook, including every game on this list. Many Chromebooks can also install Android games from the Google Play Store, as [Google's Chromebook help page](https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/7021273) explains.

### What are good free games for a Chromebook?

Slope, Run 3, 2048, Drift Boss and Fireboy and Watergirl are good free picks because they run in a browser tab and use only the keyboard. Chess and Idle Breakout are good choices if you prefer slower games.

### Do I need to download games on a Chromebook?

No. Browser games like the ones above open in a tab and start right away, with no download, install or account.

### What Chromebook games can two people play on one computer?

Fireboy and Watergirl and Basket Random both let two players share one keyboard, and Chess has a two-player mode. Each player uses their own side of the keyboard.

### Why is a game slow on my Chromebook?

Usually because the game is 3D or too many tabs are open. Close other tabs, reload the game, or switch to a lighter 2D game like 2048 or Drift Boss.

### Can I play games on a school Chromebook?

It depends on your school. School-managed Chromebooks and school networks often block game sites, and that's up to the school's rules, so check what your school allows before you play.
